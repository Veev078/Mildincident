## 1. Product and editorial vision

MILDINCIDENT is an archival, visually compelling Instagram micro-publication about unusual true stories, overlooked cultural history, photography, fashion and subcultures, unusual objects, film, nightlife, design, weird science, and internet culture. It should feel like a distinctive editorial magazine / creative production workspace, **not** a generic marketing dashboard.

Editorial principles:
- Source-led, fact-checked narratives; striking original documentary/archival imagery; strong hooks and tight captions.
- Each story has a research dossier, original-source links, media candidates, visual decisions, slide order, captions, music direction, export, Buffer handoff, and an eventual published archive.
- Prioritize substance, image quality, visual cohesion, caption strength, and archival provenance over volume. Avoid fake archival images and generic stock photos represented as originals.
- Copyright/permission is **per image**. An image being on an archive, appearing in Google/Brave, being downloadable, or being privately uploaded does **not** automatically grant publication rights. Keep research/previews/private drafts separate from publication clearance.
- Instagram carousel drafts currently target JPGs at **1080 × 1350 (4:5)**, with framing/layout rules and ordered slides.
- Instagram account **@mildincident**. Buffer is the connected scheduling tool. The user has contemplated three recurring slots around **9:00 AM, 6:30 PM, and 9:15 PM ET**, but these are preferences, **not permission** to schedule or publish on their behalf.
- User wants concise, accessible progress reports and to focus on publishing, not endless backend/tool configuration. Make reasonable engineering decisions autonomously; escalate only material tradeoffs. Never invent success.
